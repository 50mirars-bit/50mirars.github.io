Sunglasses

More Than an Accessory. A Statement of Style.

Sunglasses are more than something you wear to shield your eyes from the sun. They are one of the first details people notice, an expression of personality, confidence, and individual style. The shape of the frame, the colour of the lenses, the finish of the details, and the way a pair sits on the face all contribute to its character. The right sunglasses can transform a simple outfit, sharpen a sophisticated look, or add an effortless sense of confidence to everyday style. They are not simply an accessory — they are part of the way you present yourself.

At 50 Mirars, we believe that choosing sunglasses should be about finding a pair that feels naturally suited to you. Our collection brings together distinctive designs ranging from timeless and understated silhouettes to bold, contemporary frames made to stand out. Each pair is selected with an appreciation for proportion, design, versatility, and the details that give sunglasses their individual character. Whether you're looking for something refined for a polished occasion, a versatile pair for everyday wear, or a statement frame that commands attention, our collection is designed to complement different personalities and styles.

A great pair of sunglasses has the ability to become instantly recognizable as part of your look. It can add character to a classic outfit, bring a modern edge to your everyday wardrobe, or become the finishing touch that brings everything together. That is why we look beyond simply how a frame looks — we consider its shape, proportions, finishes, colours, and overall presence. From classic-inspired designs to contemporary statement pieces, our collection is created for those who appreciate sunglasses that feel intentional rather than ordinary.

Discover the pair that doesn't simply complete your look, but becomes a part of your style.
