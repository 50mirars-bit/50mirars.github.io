Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Your Privacy. Our Responsibility.

At 50 Mirars, we respect the privacy of every customer and website visitor. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and maintaining a secure, transparent, and trustworthy shopping experience. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, how that information may be used, how we work to protect it, and the choices available to you when using our website and services.

01. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

At 50 Mirars, we value the trust placed in us by our customers. Our website is designed to provide customers with access to our collections of watches and sunglasses while maintaining respect for their personal information. When you browse our website, contact us, enquire about a product, or place an order, certain information may be provided to us voluntarily. This Privacy Policy explains how such information may be collected, used, stored, and protected in connection with our business operations. By accessing or using the 50 Mirars website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. We encourage customers to review this page periodically because our practices or website features may change as our business develops.

02. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with 50 Mirars, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This may include your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, billing information, order information, product enquiries, and other details that may be necessary to process a purchase or respond to your request. If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, email, or another communication channel, we may receive the information that you choose to provide through that platform. Our website may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website usage information. Such information may help us understand how visitors use our website and improve its functionality, performance, security, and overall customer experience.

03. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to 50 Mirars may be used for legitimate business and customer-service purposes. This may include processing and confirming orders, arranging deliveries, responding to product enquiries, communicating with customers, providing order-related updates, handling customer-support requests, maintaining business records, and improving our website and services. We may also use information to communicate relevant product updates, new collections, promotional offers, or other business announcements where appropriate. We aim to collect and use information only for purposes that are reasonably connected to our business activities and the services requested by customers. We do not intend to use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason or, where required, the relevant permission.

04. Protection & Security of Your Information

50 Mirars takes reasonable measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to customer information may be limited to situations where it is reasonably necessary for processing orders, providing customer support, operating our website, or fulfilling other legitimate business requirements. Where third-party service providers are involved in areas such as delivery, payment processing, communication, website technology, or other operational services, certain information may need to be shared with them to the extent necessary for providing the requested service. We do not intend to sell or commercially trade customers' personal information. However, no website, online communication system, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should therefore avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through public or unsecured communication channels.

05. Cookies, Third-Party Services & Your Rights

The 50 Mirars website may use cookies or similar technologies where required to support website functionality, improve performance, understand general visitor activity, and enhance the browsing experience. Certain links or features on our website may connect you with third-party platforms or services, including communication and social-media platforms such as WhatsApp and Instagram. These third-party platforms operate under their own terms, policies, and privacy practices, and 50 Mirars does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services. Depending on applicable law, customers may have rights relating to personal information held by us, including requesting clarification, correction, or deletion of certain information. If you have a question, concern, or request regarding your personal information or this Privacy Policy, you may contact 50 Mirars through our official communication channels.

Important Privacy Notice

This Privacy Policy is intended to explain the general privacy practices of 50 Mirars. It should be reviewed and updated when required to reflect changes in our website, business operations, services, or applicable legal requirements.
