Rolex

A Legacy of Excellence. A Statement of Prestige.

A Rolex is more than a watch. It represents a distinctive sense of style, confidence, and timeless appeal. From its instantly recognizable design to the intricate details of its dial, case, bracelet, and bezel, every element contributes to the character of the timepiece.

At 50 Mirars, our Rolex collection brings together a selection of distinctive designs inspired by some of the most recognizable Rolex styles. From sophisticated Datejust designs and iconic Daytona-inspired chronographs to bold GMT and Yacht-Master styles, each piece has been selected for its presence, detailing, and timeless character.

Whether you prefer a refined everyday timepiece, a bold chronograph, or a statement design that naturally attracts attention, our collection offers a variety of styles to complement different personalities and occasions.
