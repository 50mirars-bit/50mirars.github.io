ORIGINS

INTRODUCTION

Few objects in the modern luxury canon command the mystical aura, mechanical reverence, and market frenzy of the Rolex Cosmograph Daytona. Introduced in 1963 as Reference 6239, it was crafted for professional endurance drivers.

The genesis is tied to the hard-packed sands of Daytona Beach, Florida, where land-speed pioneers shattered records while wearing Rolex Oyster cases.

CHAPTER II — HOROLOGICAL ART

THE PAUL NEWMAN ERA

During its initial manual-wind era (Calibres 72B, 722, and 727), the watch was far from an instant hit. Retailers often struggled to move inventory as clients favored slim gold dress timepieces over utilitarian steel timing devices.

Everything changed when Joanne Woodward gifted Paul Newman an exotic-dial Reference 6239 inscribed "Drive Carefully Me". Newman wore the piece throughout his racing career, converting an unpopular reference into the ultimate collector Grail.

In October 2017, Paul Newman's personal 6239 set auction history in New York, fetching $17.75 million and sealing the Daytona's status as modern fine art.

CHAPTER III — MECHANICAL MASTERY

CALIBRE 4131

In 2023, Rolex introduced Calibre 4131 featuring a Chronergy escapement, Paraflex shock absorbers, and refined Rolex Côtes de Genève bridges visible through sapphire casebacks on select models.

06

AN ENDURING ICON OF TIME

The contemporary Daytona is as much an exploration of metallurgical technology as micro-mechanics. Central to its modern aesthetic is the Cerachrom ceramic bezel insert.

Virtually scratchproof and impervious to UV radiation, Cerachrom preserves the legibility of the tachymeter scale for generations without patina or degradation.

Constructed in proprietary 904L Oystersteel, Everose gold, or 950 platinum, it remains the standard of mechanical chronographs.
