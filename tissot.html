TISSOT’S HISTORY AND HERITAGE

Founded in 1853 in the heart of Switzerland, Tissot has been at the forefront of watchmaking innovation for over 170 years. The brand’s journey began in Le Locle, where it quickly gained a reputation for crafting watches that combined technical mastery with artistic design. Throughout its history, Tissot has introduced groundbreaking advancements, such as the first mass-produced pocket watch and pioneering the use of unconventional materials. Now a proud member of the Swatch Group, Tissot continues to set benchmarks in the industry, producing watches that embody timeless elegance and sophistication. This rich heritage is reflected in every Tissot timepiece, making them a favourite among women who value style, innovation, and the enduring allure of Swiss craftsmanship.
