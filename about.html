About Us

Timeless Watches. Refined Style. Confident You.

More Than Just Watches & Sunglasses

50 Mirars is built around a simple belief: the right accessory can become an important part of the way you express yourself. We bring together watches and sunglasses that are designed to complement different personalities, occasions, and everyday moments. From a refined timepiece that completes a formal outfit to a stylish pair of sunglasses that adds confidence to a casual look, our purpose is to make great-looking accessories easier to discover. Every piece represents our focus on style, presentation, and the details that make an overall appearance feel complete.

01. Our Story

50 Mirars was created from a passion for watches, sunglasses, fashion, and the small details that can make a big difference in personal style. We believe accessories should do more than simply complete an outfit; they should reflect personality, confidence, taste, and individuality. Our journey is focused on bringing together carefully selected designs that allow customers to explore different looks and discover pieces that feel right for them. Whether someone prefers a classic and understated appearance or a bold and modern statement, we want 50 Mirars to be a place where those different styles can be explored. As our brand continues to grow, our goal remains to create a shopping experience that feels simple, professional, and genuinely enjoyable while maintaining the premium visual identity that represents 50 Mirars.

02. Our Watches

Watches are at the heart of 50 Mirars. We believe a well-chosen timepiece can become much more than something worn simply to tell the time. It can become part of someone's everyday identity and can add character to an entire outfit. Our collection brings together different designs, from classic and elegant styles to contemporary, bold, and statement-making pieces. Whether you are searching for an everyday watch, something for a formal occasion, a special celebration, or simply another addition to your collection, our aim is to provide a variety of styles from which you can choose. We pay attention to the appearance and overall presentation of the watches we feature because we want every customer to have the opportunity to find a timepiece that matches their individual taste and personal sense of style.

03. Our Sunglasses

At 50 Mirars, sunglasses are an equally important part of personal style. The right pair can change the character of an entire look, whether you want timeless elegance, modern fashion, minimal sophistication, or a confident statement. Our sunglasses collection is focused on offering different shapes, designs, and visual identities so customers can explore options that suit their preferences. We believe eyewear should feel natural as part of your everyday appearance while also giving you the confidence to stand out. Whether you are heading out casually, dressing for a special occasion, or simply looking for a new accessory, our goal is to make discovering a suitable pair of sunglasses simple and enjoyable. Just like our watches, our sunglasses are an important part of the 50 Mirars approach to complete and elevate personal style.

04. Our Approach to Quality & Style

Our approach at 50 Mirars is centered around product selection, presentation, communication, and customer experience. We understand that choosing a watch or pair of sunglasses is often a personal decision, which is why we aim to present our products clearly and make the shopping process as straightforward as possible. We continuously explore different designs and styles to keep our collection interesting and relevant to customers with different preferences. Alongside product selection, we believe responsive customer service is an important part of building a strong relationship with customers. Whether you are asking about a particular product, looking for a specific style, or simply exploring our collection, we aim to provide clear assistance and make the overall experience convenient from the first enquiry through the purchase process.

05. Our Vision for the Future

Our vision for 50 Mirars is to continue growing as a destination for watches and sunglasses while building a brand that customers remember for its style, presentation, service, and attention to detail. We want to continue expanding our collection with new designs while creating an online shopping experience that feels modern, premium, and accessible. Most importantly, we want customers to feel confident about the accessories they choose and enjoy making them part of their personal style. As 50 Mirars grows, our commitment will remain focused on bringing together watches and sunglasses that help people express their individuality, discover new styles, and make every look feel a little more complete. We believe that a brand is built not only through its products, but also through the trust and experience it creates with every customer.

The 50 Mirars Philosophy

We believe style is personal, confidence is timeless, and the right accessory can make a lasting impression. 50 Mirars — where every detail matters.
