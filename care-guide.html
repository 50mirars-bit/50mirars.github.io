Luxury Care Guide

Every timepiece tells a story, and every pair of sunglasses reflects a lifestyle. Learn how to preserve the elegance, comfort, and premium finish of your 50 Mirars collection with our complete luxury care handbook.

1. The Art of Caring for Luxury Accessories

Luxury accessories deserve more than ordinary care—they deserve thoughtful attention. A premium watch or an elegant pair of sunglasses becomes part of your daily lifestyle, collecting memories with every wear. By cleaning them regularly, storing them safely, and protecting them from unnecessary damage, you preserve not only their appearance but also their lasting elegance. Simple habits practiced consistently will ensure your collection continues looking refined for years to come.

2. Keep Your Watch Crystal Clean

After wearing your watch throughout the day, fingerprints, dust particles, and natural skin oils begin settling on the surface. Using a soft microfiber cloth removes these marks without scratching the crystal or polished metal finish. Avoid household cleaners or rough fabrics because they gradually reduce the luxurious shine that makes a premium watch stand out.

3. Respect Water Resistance

Water-resistant watches are designed for protection, but they still require sensible handling. Hot showers, steam, and sudden temperature changes can weaken seals over time. Always ensure the crown is fully secured before exposing your watch to moisture, and remember that proper care extends the life of its internal movement.

4. Store It Like a Collector

When you're not wearing your watch, store it inside a dedicated watch box or a soft protective pouch. Proper storage prevents scratches, keeps dust away, and protects delicate finishes from unnecessary wear. Collectors understand that storage is just as important as daily use.

Luxury Lives in the Details

The finest accessories remain timeless because they're cared for with intention, patience, and respect.

5. Clean Sunglasses Properly

Your sunglasses protect your eyes while completing your style. Always clean the lenses with a microfiber cloth and lens-safe cleaning solution. Paper tissues, shirts, or rough fabrics may seem harmless, but they gradually create fine scratches that reduce optical clarity.

6. Always Use a Protective Case

A premium hard case protects your sunglasses from accidental pressure, falls, and scratches while traveling. Even placing them upside down on a table can damage the lenses, so building the habit of using a protective case keeps them looking flawless.

7. Protect Against Heat

Excessive heat can warp premium frames and damage protective lens coatings. Avoid leaving sunglasses inside a parked car or directly under intense sunlight for extended periods. Cooler storage conditions help preserve both comfort and durability.

8. Handle Frames with Care

Always use both hands when putting on or removing your sunglasses. This simple habit distributes pressure evenly across the frame, preventing unnecessary bending and helping maintain the perfect fit over time.

9. Regular Maintenance Matters

Inspect screws, hinges, and moving parts every few weeks. Tightening a loose hinge early prevents larger repairs later and keeps your accessories feeling as premium as the day you received them.

10. Preserve the Luxury Experience

True luxury isn't only about buying premium products—it's about preserving them with pride. Every careful cleaning, every protective case, and every thoughtful habit ensures your 50 Mirars collection remains elegant, reliable, and timeless for years ahead.

Golden Care Checklist

✔ Clean with a microfiber cloth only.
✔ Store in a protective case.
✔ Keep away from excessive heat.
✔ Remove watches during heavy impact activities.
✔ Keep crowns securely closed.
✔ Check hinges and screws regularly.

Luxury Deserves Lasting Care

Your collection represents timeless style. Give every watch and every pair of sunglasses the attention they deserve, and they'll continue reflecting elegance for years to come.
