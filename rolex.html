ROLEX HISTORY AND HERITAGE

Founded in 1905, Rolex has played a major role in the development of modern watchmaking. The brand has consistently pushed the boundaries of precision, durability, and functionality while maintaining its unmistakable design language. Rolex introduced important innovations throughout its history, including the world's first waterproof wristwatch case and the development of highly reliable automatic movements. This heritage continues to influence every Rolex watch produced today.
