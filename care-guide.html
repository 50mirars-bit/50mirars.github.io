01

The Art of Caring for Luxury Accessories

Luxury accessories deserve thoughtful attention every single day. A premium watch or elegant pair of sunglasses becomes part of your lifestyle, collecting memories with every occasion. Caring for them preserves their beauty, confidence, and sophistication for years.

02

Keep Your Watch Crystal Clean

Fingerprints, dust, and natural oils slowly reduce the brilliance of your watch. A soft microfiber cloth keeps the crystal and bracelet polished without causing scratches or damaging protective coatings.

03

Respect Water Resistance

Water resistance has limits. Avoid unnecessary exposure to steam, hot showers, and swimming pools unless your watch is specifically designed for those conditions.

04

Store It Like a Collector

Keep your watch inside a dedicated watch box or soft pouch away from sunlight, humidity, and magnetic devices. Proper storage protects delicate finishes and polished surfaces.

Luxury Lives in the Details

The finest accessories remain timeless because they're cared for with intention, patience, and respect.

05

Clean Sunglasses Properly

Use a microfiber cloth and lens-safe cleaning solution instead of tissues or clothing. Proper cleaning preserves protective coatings and keeps your vision crystal clear.

06

Always Use a Protective Case

A premium hard case protects your sunglasses from scratches, pressure, and accidental drops while traveling or carrying them inside a bag.

07

Protect Against Heat

Extreme heat can warp premium frames and damage delicate lens coatings. Cooler storage conditions help preserve both comfort and durability.

08

Handle Frames with Care

Always use both hands when wearing or removing your sunglasses. This prevents unnecessary bending and helps maintain their perfect fit.

09

Regular Maintenance Matters

Inspect screws, hinges, and moving parts regularly. Small maintenance habits keep your accessories feeling smooth, secure, and dependable.

10

Preserve the Luxury Experience

True luxury isn't only about owning premium accessories—it's about preserving them with pride through careful cleaning, thoughtful storage, and consistent maintenance.

Golden Care Checklist

✔ Clean with a microfiber cloth only.
✔ Store products in a protective case.
✔ Keep away from excessive heat.
✔ Remove watches during heavy impact activities.
✔ Keep the crown securely closed.
✔ Check hinges and screws regularly.
