PARIS • 1847

Where Elegance Found Its Home

The story of Cartier began in Paris in 1847, when Louis-François Cartier transformed a small jewelry workshop into what would become one of the world's most celebrated luxury houses.

Long before Cartier created iconic eyewear, its artisans mastered precious metals, gemstones and refined proportions—skills that would later define every frame bearing the famous signature.

The spirit of Place Vendôme still lives inside Cartier Eyewear, where French elegance remains the foundation of every design.

THE GOLD STANDARD

When Jewelry Became Eyewear

As Cartier expanded internationally under Pierre Cartier, the Maison became synonymous with refined luxury, supplying royalty, aristocrats and influential collectors across Europe and America.

That same commitment to perfection eventually shaped Cartier Eyewear, where precious materials, elegant geometry and exceptional finishing transformed sunglasses into wearable jewelry.

Every polished gold frame feels less like an accessory and more like a carefully crafted object of timeless design.

THE PANTHER SYMBOL

An Icon of Confidence

Few luxury symbols are as instantly recognizable as Cartier's Panther. Introduced as a bold expression of strength and elegance, it became one of the Maison's most celebrated signatures.

Within Cartier Eyewear, subtle Panther-inspired details appear through sculpted temples, refined metalwork and graceful silhouettes that embody confidence without unnecessary excess.

Rather than dominating the design, these details reward those who appreciate craftsmanship hidden beneath simplicity.

"True luxury is recognized by those who understand the smallest details."
— 50 MIRARS JOURNAL
THE CRAFTSMANSHIP

Every Frame Begins Like Jewelry

Unlike ordinary sunglasses, Cartier Eyewear is created with the same attention traditionally reserved for precious jewelry. Every polished edge, engraved screw and sculpted temple reflects decades of French craftsmanship.

SANTOS COLLECTION

Inspired by Aviation. Finished Like Jewelry.

The Santos collection carries the spirit of Alberto Santos-Dumont's pioneering wristwatch into contemporary eyewear, preserving the iconic exposed screws and architectural geometry that made the original design legendary.

Rather than chasing fashion, Santos frames celebrate timeless engineering, making them instantly recognizable to collectors around the world.

The familiar screw motifs become elegant design elements that connect Cartier's watchmaking heritage with modern luxury eyewear.

PRECIOUS METALS

Luxury You Can Feel

Cartier has always understood that luxury begins with materials. Gold finishes, titanium construction and carefully polished surfaces create frames that feel remarkably refined before they're ever worn.

Even the smallest hinge receives exceptional attention, ensuring every movement feels smooth, precise and unmistakably premium.

The result is elegance that can be felt as much as it can be seen.

NATURAL MATERIALS

The Quiet Luxury of Buffalo Horn

Some Cartier creations incorporate genuine buffalo horn—a material admired for its natural warmth, distinctive patterns and remarkable comfort on the face.

Because every piece carries unique organic variations, no two frames are ever completely identical, giving each creation an individual character.

It is craftsmanship that embraces nature rather than hiding it.

THE MODERN COLLECTION

From Parisian Elegance to Global Icons

Today's Cartier Eyewear continues to balance timeless craftsmanship with contemporary confidence, making every frame feel equally at home on Parisian boulevards and modern city skylines.

CONTEMPORARY LUXURY

Timeless Without Feeling Traditional

Rather than following short-lived trends, Cartier creates frames that preserve elegant proportions while embracing modern materials and refined comfort.

The familiar balance of gold, black and sculptural lines allows every generation to discover Cartier without losing the Maison's unmistakable identity.

It is luxury that evolves without forgetting where it began.

CULTURAL INFLUENCE

The Frames Chosen by Icons

From musicians and actors to entrepreneurs and fashion leaders, Cartier Eyewear has become a symbol of understated confidence rather than loud extravagance.

The appeal lies in recognition without obvious branding—a quiet language understood by those who appreciate exceptional craftsmanship.

Every frame feels equally suited to a tailored suit, a luxury yacht or a private gallery opening.
