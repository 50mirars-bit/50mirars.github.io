Watches

More Than Time. A Statement of Style.

A watch is more than an accessory worn around the wrist. It is a reflection of personality, taste, and the attention one gives to the finer details of everyday life. From the character of the dial and the proportions of the case to the finish of the bracelet and the smallest elements of its design, every detail contributes to the identity of a timepiece. A well-chosen watch can quietly elevate an outfit, add confidence to a formal look, or become the defining detail of an everyday style. It does not simply tell you the time — it becomes part of how you present yourself to the world.

At 50 Mirars, we believe that choosing a watch should be about more than following what is currently trending. It is about discovering a timepiece that feels right for you. Our collection brings together a carefully selected range of distinctive watches, from sophisticated and understated designs to bold chronographs and statement-making timepieces. Each piece has been selected with an appreciation for design, presence, versatility, and the timeless appeal of a great watch. Whether you are looking for something refined for special occasions, a bold companion for everyday wear, or a distinctive piece that naturally draws attention, our collection offers something for every style and personality.

A truly memorable watch has a quality that goes beyond its appearance. It becomes familiar on the wrist, complements different moments, and develops its own place in your personal style. That is why we look beyond simply how a watch looks — we consider the details, proportions, finishes, colours, and overall character that make each piece distinctive. From classic silhouettes to contemporary designs, our collection is created for those who appreciate watches that feel intentional rather than ordinary.

Discover a timepiece that doesn't simply keep time, but becomes a part of yours.
