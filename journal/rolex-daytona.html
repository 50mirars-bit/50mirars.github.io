Horology & High Speed

Rolex Daytona

Born on the race track, refined in luxury. The story of how a utilitarian chronograph became the undisputed grail of watchmaking.

“Engineered to calculate average speeds up to 400 units per hour, it stands as the ultimate driver's instrument.”
— Official Rolex Cosmograph Daytona Feature
01

Motorsport DNA

Introduced in 1963, the Cosmograph Daytona was designed specifically to meet the high-stakes demands of professional racing drivers. Featuring a high-contrast dial and a signature tachymetric scale engraved directly onto its bezel, it allowed drivers to read speed calculations instantaneously at extreme velocities.

02

Cerachrom Precision

Modern Daytona models feature a high-tech Cerachrom bezel molded from high-density ceramic. It is virtually scratchproof, unaffected by UV rays, and impervious to corrosion. The engraved numerals are deposited with a microscopic layer of pure platinum or gold using an advanced PVD process.
