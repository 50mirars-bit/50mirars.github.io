THE ORIGIN

Born From the World of MotoGP

The T-Race wasn't inspired by racing from a distance—it was born within it. Created for enthusiasts who admired speed, engineering and precision, the collection became one of the clearest expressions of Tissot's long-standing relationship with motorcycle racing.

2004

The First T-Race

When Tissot introduced the T-Race in 2004, it immediately stood apart from conventional chronographs. Instead of borrowing racing colors alone, it translated real motorcycle components into watch design.

The brake-disc bezel, tire-inspired details and bold pushers created a watch that looked as though it had been engineered in a pit lane rather than a design studio.

OFFICIAL TIMEKEEPER

Timekeeper of Champions

As Official Timekeeper of MotoGP, Tissot measures races decided by thousandths of a second. That relentless pursuit of accuracy became part of the T-Race identity.

Rather than creating a watch that merely celebrated racing, Tissot built one that reflected the same precision demanded on the world's fastest circuits.

RACING HERITAGE

Where Every Millisecond Matters

On a MotoGP circuit, victory can be decided by thousandths of a second. That philosophy of relentless precision lives inside every T-Race, transforming Swiss engineering into a tribute to the world's fastest motorcycles.

DESIGN LANGUAGE

Built Like a Motorcycle, Not Just Inspired by One

The T-Race stands apart because its design borrows real mechanical ideas from high-performance motorcycles. Every visible detail serves as a reminder that this watch belongs to the world of speed.

BRAKE DISC BEZEL

The Signature Feature

The rotating bezel immediately recalls a ventilated motorcycle brake disc, one of the most recognizable components in modern racing machines.

Instead of becoming decorative, this design element gives the T-Race an unmistakable identity that enthusiasts recognize instantly.

CHRONOGRAPH PUSHERS

Inspired by Motorcycle Controls

The oversized pushers resemble the confident feel of motorcycle controls, creating an interaction that feels deliberate and mechanical rather than delicate.

Their sculpted shape reinforces the T-Race's bold character while making the chronograph satisfying to operate.

RUBBER STRAP

Grip Inspired by the Track

The textured rubber strap echoes the tread patterns found on performance motorcycle tires, improving comfort while strengthening the collection's racing identity.

It's a subtle detail that connects the watch to the machines that inspired it.

CASE ENGINEERING

Protection With Purpose

The integrated crown guards create a stronger visual profile while helping protect the chronograph controls from everyday impacts.

Combined with angular lugs and muscular proportions, they give the T-Race the confident stance of a superbike waiting on the starting grid.

THE MODERN COLLECTION

Born From Racing. Built For Today.

Today's Tissot T-Race collection proves that motorsport-inspired design can evolve without losing its original spirit. From MotoGP editions to everyday chronographs, each model carries the unmistakable DNA of speed.

MOTOGP EDITION

The Official Racing Spirit

Modern MotoGP editions continue celebrating Tissot's partnership with motorcycle racing through engraved casebacks, racing-inspired details and limited-edition presentation boxes.

Every detail reminds collectors that this watch isn't merely inspired by racing—it's part of the sport itself.

QUARTZ CHRONOGRAPH

Precision Without Compromise

The quartz chronograph version delivers outstanding everyday reliability while preserving the aggressive case design that made the T-Race instantly recognizable.

For many enthusiasts, it offers the perfect combination of Swiss precision and effortless practicality.

AUTOMATIC MOVEMENT

The Mechanical Heart

Automatic versions reveal an entirely different personality. Instead of chasing split-second timing electronically, they celebrate the beautiful rhythm of gears, springs and handcrafted engineering.

The visible movement reminds owners that racing isn't only about speed—it's about mechanical excellence.
