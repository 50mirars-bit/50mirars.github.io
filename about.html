More Than Just Watches & Sunglasses

50 Mirars is built around a simple idea: the right accessory can become a defining part of your personal style. We bring together watches and sunglasses designed to complement different personalities, occasions, and everyday moments. From a sophisticated timepiece that completes a formal look to a stylish pair of sunglasses that adds confidence to a casual outfit, our aim is to make premium-looking style more accessible while keeping the experience simple, personal, and enjoyable.

01. Our Story

50 Mirars was created with a passion for watches, sunglasses, fashion, and the small details that make an appearance feel complete. We believe that a watch is more than an instrument for telling time and that sunglasses are more than simply an accessory. Both can express personality, confidence, taste, and individuality. Our journey is focused on bringing together carefully selected styles that allow customers to discover pieces that feel right for them. As we continue to grow, our vision remains centered on offering an enjoyable shopping experience where customers can explore different designs, styles, and price points without losing the sense of sophistication that defines 50 Mirars.

02. Our Watches

Watches are at the heart of 50 Mirars. We believe a well-chosen timepiece can instantly elevate an outfit and become an important part of someone's everyday identity. Our collection is focused on offering a variety of designs, from classic and understated styles to bold, contemporary, and statement-making pieces. Whether you are looking for something suitable for everyday wear, a formal occasion, a special celebration, or simply a new addition to your collection, we aim to provide options that combine visual appeal with a strong sense of style. Every watch is selected with the intention of giving our customers more choices when it comes to expressing their individual personality through what they wear on their wrist.

03. Our Sunglasses

At 50 Mirars, sunglasses are an equally important part of personal style. A great pair of sunglasses can change the character of an entire look, whether the goal is timeless elegance, modern fashion, minimal sophistication, or a bold statement. Our sunglasses collection is selected to offer different shapes, styles, and visual identities so that customers can find something that suits their preferences. We believe sunglasses should feel stylish and confident while fitting naturally into everyday life. From relaxed outings to occasions where appearance matters most, our goal is to make it easier for customers to discover eyewear that complements their personality and completes their overall look.

04. Our Approach to Quality & Style

Our approach at 50 Mirars is centered on selection, presentation, and customer experience. We pay attention to the overall appearance of the products we feature because we understand that customers want their accessories to look distinctive and feel worth choosing. We continuously explore different designs and styles to keep our collection interesting and relevant. Alongside product selection, we believe that clear communication and responsive customer service are important parts of a good shopping experience. Whether a customer is asking about a product, looking for a particular style, or placing an order, we aim to make the process straightforward and convenient from the first enquiry to the final purchase.

05. Our Vision for the Future

Our vision for 50 Mirars is to continue growing as a trusted destination for watches and sunglasses while building a brand that customers remember for its style, presentation, service, and attention to detail. We want to keep expanding our collection with new designs and continue creating a shopping experience that feels modern, premium, and accessible. Most importantly, we want every customer to feel confident about the piece they choose. As 50 Mirars grows, our commitment will remain the same: to bring together watches and sunglasses that help people express their individuality and make every look feel a little more complete.

The 50 Mirars Philosophy

We believe style is personal, confidence is timeless, and the right accessory can make a lasting impression. 50 Mirars — where every detail matters.
