01

The Art of Caring for Luxury Accessories

Luxury accessories are more than everyday essentials—they become a reflection of your personality, confidence, and the memorable moments you experience throughout life. Every premium watch records valuable moments, while every pair of sunglasses accompanies journeys, celebrations, and everyday adventures with effortless elegance. Caring for these accessories is about preserving craftsmanship, precision, and premium finishes through thoughtful habits such as proper cleaning, careful storage, and gentle handling.

02

Keep Your Watch Crystal Clean

The crystal is one of the most beautiful features of a luxury watch. Fingerprints, dust, and skin oils gradually reduce its brilliance throughout the day. Use a clean microfiber cloth to gently wipe the crystal, bezel, and bracelet, while avoiding tissues or harsh cleaners that may create microscopic scratches. This simple routine preserves the refined elegance of your timepiece.

03

Respect Water Resistance

Water resistance is designed to protect your watch, but hot showers, steam, and swimming pools can gradually weaken protective seals. Always ensure the crown is fully secured before water exposure, and gently dry your watch afterward with a microfiber cloth. Respecting these limits helps preserve accuracy, reliability, and timeless luxury.

04

Store It Like a Collector

The way you store your watch is just as important as the way you wear it. Experienced collectors understand that proper storage protects both beauty and performance. Instead of leaving your watch on a bedside table or inside a crowded drawer, keep it inside a dedicated watch box or a soft protective pouch whenever it is not being worn. Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, excessive humidity, and strong magnetic devices that may affect certain movements over time. Giving every watch its own space helps preserve polished finishes, delicate edges, and premium materials while ensuring it remains ready for every important occasion with timeless elegance.

Luxury Lives in the Details

The finest accessories remain timeless because they're cared for with intention, patience, and respect.

05

Clean Sunglasses Properly

Premium sunglasses combine elegant design with advanced lens technology, making proper cleaning one of the most important habits for preserving their luxurious appearance. Remove loose dust first to avoid scratching the lenses, then use a clean microfiber cloth together with a lens-safe cleaning solution. Avoid shirts, paper tissues, or harsh household cleaners because they can damage delicate protective coatings. A careful cleaning routine keeps your lenses crystal clear while maintaining the refined appearance that defines premium eyewear.

06

Always Use a Protective Case

A protective case is one of the simplest yet most valuable investments you can make for your premium sunglasses. Even high-quality frames remain vulnerable to scratches, pressure, and accidental drops when left unprotected inside a bag or placed carelessly on a table. A hard protective case absorbs impact while protecting delicate hinges, temples, and premium lenses. Fold your sunglasses carefully before storing them, and this small habit will help preserve their alignment, comfort, and luxurious finish for years.

07

Protect Against Heat

Excessive heat is one of the most overlooked threats to premium sunglasses because it can slowly affect both the frame and the lenses without causing immediate visible damage. Leaving your sunglasses inside a parked car, placing them near heating vents, or exposing them to intense sunlight for long periods may gradually warp the frame and weaken delicate lens coatings. Whenever possible, keep them inside their protective case and store them in a cool, shaded environment to preserve their original shape, optical clarity, and premium appearance.

08

Handle Frames with Care

Luxury sunglasses are engineered for both comfort and elegant styling, which is why proper handling makes a noticeable difference over time. Always use both hands when putting on or removing your sunglasses to distribute pressure evenly across the frame. Removing them with one hand repeatedly can gradually loosen hinges, affect alignment, and reduce the comfortable fit that premium eyewear is designed to provide. Keep the lenses facing upward whenever they are placed on a surface, and dry them gently before returning them to their protective case after exposure to rain or perspiration.

09

Regular Maintenance Matters

Regular maintenance is one of the easiest ways to keep luxury accessories performing and looking their best because small checks today often prevent larger problems tomorrow. Inspect your watches and sunglasses every few weeks for loose screws, worn hinges, hidden dust, or other signs of everyday wear. Tightening a loose hinge early can prevent unnecessary repairs, while professional servicing after years of regular use helps preserve precision, comfort, and long-term performance.

10

Preserve the Luxury Experience

True luxury is not defined only by owning premium accessories—it is defined by preserving them with pride, appreciation, and thoughtful care throughout every stage of their journey. Every careful cleaning routine, every protective case, every proper storage habit, and every small maintenance check contributes to a collection that continues looking elegant year after year. At 50 Mirars, we believe every premium accessory deserves care that matches the quality with which it was created, allowing timeless elegance to accompany you every single day.

Golden Care Checklist

✔ Clean with a microfiber cloth only.
✔ Store products in a protective case.
