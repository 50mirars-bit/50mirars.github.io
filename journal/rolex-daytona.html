Rolex Cosmograph Daytona

Designed to Drive

The Iconic Motorsport Chronograph

Introduced in 1963, the Cosmograph Daytona was designed to meet the demands of professional racing drivers. With its highly reliable chronograph and bezel featuring a tachymetric scale, it allows drivers to perfectly measure average speeds up to 400 kilometers or miles per hour. An icon eternally joined in name and function to the high-performance world of motorsport.

Precision & Legendary Design

At the core of the Daytona’s identity is its tachymetric bezel, crafted from high-tech materials like Cerachrom ceramic. This key feature offers excellent legibility and remains virtually scratchproof. Paired with the high-performance Calibre 4131 movement, the Daytona guarantees exceptional precision, robustness, and an impressive power reserve.
