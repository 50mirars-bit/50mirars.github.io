01

The Art of Caring for Luxury Accessories

Luxury accessories are more than everyday essentials—they become a reflection of your personality, confidence, and the memorable moments you experience throughout life. Every premium watch records valuable moments, while every pair of sunglasses accompanies journeys, celebrations, and everyday adventures with effortless elegance. Caring for these accessories is not simply about maintaining their appearance; it is about preserving the craftsmanship, precision, and premium finish that make them special. Small habits such as wiping away fingerprints after use, storing each piece carefully, and protecting them from unnecessary damage help maintain their beauty for years. High-quality materials are designed to last, but their longevity depends greatly on consistent care and thoughtful handling. By dedicating just a few minutes to proper maintenance, you protect polished surfaces, delicate movements, premium lenses, and beautifully finished frames from everyday wear. At 50 Mirars, we believe luxury should continue feeling extraordinary long after the first purchase, and this guide has been created to help every customer preserve that timeless experience.

02

Keep Your Watch Crystal Clean

The crystal of a luxury watch is one of its most striking features, allowing the beauty of the dial to shine with perfect clarity every time you check the time. Throughout the day, fingerprints, dust, natural skin oils, and tiny airborne particles gradually settle on the crystal, bezel, and bracelet, reducing their brilliant finish without you even noticing. A soft microfiber cloth should become part of your daily routine, as it gently removes these marks without scratching delicate surfaces or damaging protective coatings. Avoid household cleaners, rough fabrics, or paper tissues because they may create microscopic scratches that become visible over time. If your watch has a stainless-steel bracelet, gently clean between the links using a soft brush to remove hidden dirt. This simple one-minute routine preserves reflections, keeps polished surfaces looking flawless, and allows your timepiece to maintain the refined elegance that defines every premium watch from the very first day you wear it.

03

Respect Water Resistance

Water resistance is one of the most valuable features of a luxury watch, but understanding its limits is essential for protecting the movement inside. Many premium watches comfortably handle light rain or everyday splashes, yet unnecessary exposure to hot showers, steam rooms, swimming pools, or sudden temperature changes can slowly weaken the protective seals that keep moisture away from delicate internal components. Before any contact with water, always make sure the crown is fully pushed in or securely screwed down according to your watch's design. After accidental exposure, gently dry the case and bracelet using a soft microfiber cloth instead of allowing moisture to remain around the crown or lugs. Saltwater and chlorinated water deserve extra attention because they can leave residue if not cleaned properly afterward. Treating water resistance as a feature to respect rather than test will help preserve accuracy, reliability, and the luxurious appearance that makes your watch a timeless companion.

04

Store It Like a Collector

The way you store your watch is just as important as the way you wear it, and experienced collectors understand that proper storage protects both beauty and performance. Leaving a premium watch on a bedside table, inside a crowded drawer, or alongside other accessories increases the risk of scratches, dust buildup, and accidental impacts. Instead, place your watch inside a dedicated watch box or a soft protective pouch whenever it is not being worn. Choose a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, excessive humidity, and strong magnetic devices that may affect certain movements over time. If you own multiple watches, give each piece enough space to avoid unnecessary contact between bracelets and cases. A thoughtful storage routine helps preserve polished finishes, delicate edges, and premium materials while ensuring your timepiece remains ready for every important occasion with the same elegance it had on the first day.

05

Clean Sunglasses Properly

Premium sunglasses combine elegant design with advanced lens technology, making proper cleaning one of the most important habits for preserving their luxurious appearance. Throughout the day, fingerprints, dust, sunscreen residue, and tiny airborne particles naturally settle on the lenses, reducing clarity if they are not removed correctly. Before cleaning, gently remove loose dust to prevent it from scratching the surface, then use a clean microfiber cloth together with a lens-safe cleaning solution. Avoid wiping the lenses with your shirt, paper tissues, or rough fabrics because these materials can create microscopic scratches that become noticeable over time. Harsh household cleaners should also be avoided since they may damage protective coatings designed to reduce glare and improve visibility. By taking a few moments to clean your sunglasses properly after regular use, you preserve crystal-clear vision, maintain vibrant reflections, and keep every pair looking as refined as the day it joined your collection.

06

Always Use a Protective Case

A protective case is one of the simplest yet most valuable investments you can make for your premium sunglasses because it protects them from everyday accidents that often happen unexpectedly. Even high-quality frames remain vulnerable to scratches, pressure, and accidental drops when left unprotected inside a bag or placed carelessly on a table. A hard protective case absorbs impact while preventing unnecessary stress on delicate hinges, temples, and premium lenses during travel or daily use. Whenever you remove your sunglasses, fold them carefully and place them back inside their case instead of leaving them exposed to dust or accidental contact with other objects. This small habit protects lens coatings, keeps the frame perfectly aligned, and helps preserve the luxurious appearance that makes premium eyewear feel truly special. Consistent use of a protective case ensures your sunglasses continue looking elegant and comfortable for years of everyday wear.

07

Protect Against Heat

Excessive heat is one of the most overlooked threats to premium sunglasses because it can slowly affect both the frame and the lenses without causing immediate visible damage. Leaving your sunglasses inside a parked car, placing them near heating vents, or exposing them to intense sunlight for long periods may gradually warp the frame and weaken delicate lens coatings that improve visual comfort. Even small temperature changes repeated over time can reduce durability if proper care is ignored. Whenever possible, keep your sunglasses inside their protective case and store them in a cool, shaded environment rather than leaving them exposed unnecessarily. These simple precautions help maintain the original shape of the frame, preserve optical clarity, and ensure your premium eyewear continues delivering the same luxurious experience every time you wear it. Protecting against heat requires very little effort but provides lasting benefits for years ahead.

08

Handle Frames with Care

Luxury sunglasses are carefully engineered to provide both comfort and elegant styling, which is why proper handling makes a noticeable difference over time. One of the easiest habits to develop is using both hands whenever you put on or remove your sunglasses because this distributes pressure evenly across the frame. Removing them with one hand repeatedly can gradually loosen hinges, affect alignment, and reduce the comfortable fit that premium eyewear is designed to provide. Whenever you place your sunglasses on a surface, always keep the lenses facing upward to avoid unnecessary scratches, and if they become wet from rain or perspiration, dry them gently before returning them to their case. These small daily habits preserve comfort, maintain perfect alignment, and help your eyewear continue reflecting the craftsmanship and elegance that define every luxury accessory from 50 Mirars.

09

Regular Maintenance Matters

Regular maintenance is one of the easiest ways to keep luxury accessories performing and looking their best because small checks today often prevent larger problems tomorrow. Every few weeks, inspect your watches and sunglasses carefully for loose screws, worn hinges, hidden dust, or other signs of everyday wear before they become more noticeable. Tightening a loose hinge early can prevent unnecessary repairs, while cleaning hidden areas helps preserve polished finishes and smooth operation. For watches, ensure the crown operates comfortably and the bracelet remains secure without unnecessary movement. For sunglasses, check that the temples open smoothly and sit evenly on both sides. Professional servicing becomes especially valuable after years of regular use because trained technicians can inspect internal components that cannot be seen from the outside. Consistent maintenance protects your investment while ensuring every premium piece continues delivering the same confidence and elegance that made it special from the very beginning.
