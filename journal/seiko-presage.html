THE ORIGIN

From Japan's First Wristwatch to a Modern Classic

The story of Seiko Presage begins long before the collection itself. It begins with a bold ambition—to create timepieces that could stand proudly beside the finest watches in the world while remaining deeply rooted in Japanese craftsmanship.

1913

The Laurel Changed Everything

In 1913, Seiko introduced the Laurel—the first wristwatch ever produced in Japan. At a time when pocket watches dominated the world, this pioneering creation marked the beginning of a new chapter in Japanese watchmaking.

Its enamel dial, elegant proportions and handcrafted details established principles that would continue to influence Seiko's philosophy for generations.

MODERN ERA

The Birth of Presage

When Seiko introduced the Presage collection internationally, it wasn't simply launching another watch—it was presenting Japanese artistry to the global luxury market.

Each mechanical movement, polished case and beautifully finished dial reflected the belief that true luxury lies in craftsmanship rather than excess.

THE ART OF THE DIAL

Where Centuries of Craftsmanship Meet Every Second

The true beauty of Seiko Presage lies not only in its mechanical movements but in the remarkable artistry found on every dial. Inspired by Japanese traditions that have existed for generations, these watches transform craftsmanship into something that can be worn every day.

ENAMEL DIAL

Fired Like Fine Porcelain

Creating an enamel dial requires extraordinary patience. Multiple layers of enamel are carefully applied before being fired at high temperatures, producing a surface with remarkable depth and lasting brilliance.

The result is a dial that feels timeless—its rich white glow remaining beautiful decades after it leaves the workshop.

URUSHI LACQUER

The Beauty of Japanese Lacquer

Urushi is one of Japan's oldest artistic traditions, using natural lacquer that develops extraordinary depth through repeated layers and careful polishing.

Within the Presage collection, this centuries-old craft transforms the dial into a living work of art, where changing light reveals subtle reflections throughout the day.

ARITA PORCELAIN

400 Years of Ceramic Excellence

Produced using techniques developed in the historic town of Arita, these porcelain dials celebrate one of Japan's most celebrated artistic traditions.

Every dial carries a unique warmth that combines traditional craftsmanship with modern durability, creating one of the most distinctive surfaces in luxury watchmaking.

COCKTAIL TIME

Inspired by Light in Motion

Perhaps the most famous Presage collection, Cocktail Time captures the shimmering reflections of expertly crafted cocktails through beautifully textured dials.

As light moves across the surface, the dial appears almost alive—changing character from every angle while preserving the elegant simplicity that defines Japanese design.

THE MODERN COLLECTION

Classic Craftsmanship for Today's Collector

The modern Presage family proves that timeless design never grows old. Whether inspired by Japanese cocktails, geometric architecture or open-heart mechanics, every model preserves the same philosophy—craftsmanship before extravagance.

COCKTAIL TIME

The Collection That Captured Light

The Cocktail Time series became one of Seiko's most celebrated creations by transforming the shimmering reflections of expertly crafted cocktails into remarkable dial textures.

As light moves across the surface, the dial appears almost alive, creating an experience that feels different every time it's worn.

SHARP EDGED SERIES

Geometry Meets Precision

Inspired by traditional Japanese hemp-leaf patterns, the Sharp Edged Series combines bold architecture with refined finishing, offering a more contemporary interpretation of Presage elegance.

Its angular case, textured dial and polished surfaces create a striking balance between heritage and modern luxury.

OPEN HEART

The Beauty of Mechanical Motion

The Open Heart collection reveals the rhythmic heartbeat of the movement, allowing wearers to appreciate the craftsmanship usually hidden beneath the dial.

Rather than overwhelming the design, this elegant opening celebrates mechanical artistry with remarkable restraint.
