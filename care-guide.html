Luxury Care Guide

Preserve the elegance of every timepiece and every pair of sunglasses. Follow our expert care guide to keep your 50 Mirars collection looking premium for years.

Welcome to the Luxury Care Guide

Your watch and sunglasses are more than accessories—they're an expression of your personality. With proper care, they'll stay elegant, comfortable, and beautiful for years. This guide has been created by 50 Mirars to help you protect your premium collection.

1. Clean Your Watch Regularly

Wipe your watch gently with a soft microfiber cloth after every use. Removing fingerprints, dust, and sweat helps maintain the polished finish and keeps your timepiece looking new.

2. Protect Against Water Damage

Even water-resistant watches should avoid hot showers, steam, and unnecessary exposure to water. Always make sure the crown is fully closed before any contact with moisture.

3. Store Your Watch Properly

Store your watch inside a protective watch box or soft pouch whenever you're not wearing it. Avoid direct sunlight and humid places.

4. Avoid Strong Impacts

Luxury watches contain delicate movements. Remove your watch before gym workouts, heavy lifting, or sports that may expose it to shocks.

5. Service Your Watch

Regular servicing keeps your watch accurate and extends its lifespan. Professional maintenance helps protect the internal movement.

6. Clean Lenses Correctly

Always use a microfiber cloth to clean your sunglasses. Avoid tissues, paper towels, or clothing because they can scratch the lenses.

7. Use a Protective Case

Keep your sunglasses inside a hard protective case whenever they're not being worn. This prevents accidental damage.

8. Avoid Extreme Heat

Never leave your sunglasses inside a hot car for long periods. Excessive heat can warp frames and damage lens coatings.

9. Handle Frames Properly

Always use both hands when putting on or removing your sunglasses. This helps maintain frame alignment.

10. Regular Maintenance

Inspect hinges and screws occasionally. Tightening loose parts early prevents bigger problems later.

11. Enjoy Your Luxury Collection

Premium accessories deserve premium care. With these simple habits, your watches and sunglasses will continue looking elegant for years to come.

Golden Care Checklist

  • ✔ Use a microfiber cloth only.
  • ✔ Store products in a protective case.
  • ✔ Keep away from extreme heat.
  • ✔ Remove watches during heavy impact activities.
  • ✔ Keep watches dry whenever possible.
  • ✔ Check hinges and screws regularly.

Luxury deserves lasting care.

Protect your timepieces and sunglasses with the same attention they were designed with.
